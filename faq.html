Frequently asked questions

A little clarity.
Before your visit.

Useful details for planning your time at SB Fusion.

Where is SB Fusion located?+

Find us on the ground floor of Astra Tower, North Tower, opposite the VIVO service centre in New Town, Kolkata 700161. Use the directions link to plan your route.

Is this a unisex salon?+

Yes. SB Fusion is a family salon offering hair and beauty services for men and women. Ask the salon about the service you have in mind.

How do I enquire about an appointment?+

Call +91 62895 78732 and share your service and preferred date. Your appointment is confirmed only when the salon confirms the details.

Can I check prices before visiting?+

Yes. Call the salon for the current price of your chosen service, including any options or extras, before agreeing to it.

Which hair services can I ask about?+

Ask about cuts and styling, hair colour, conditioning and hair spa, smoothening or straightening. Discuss the look you want and your hair’s previous treatment history.

Do you offer nail care and occasion makeup?+

Nail care, manicures, pedicures, nail art and makeup are listed among the salon’s services. Call to check availability for your preferred date.

What are the opening hours?+

Call before travelling to confirm the day’s opening hours and availability.

Are the photos recent?+

The gallery brings together photographs from the salon’s public listing. They show past salon moments and looks; ask about current services, staff and arrangements when planning a visit.
